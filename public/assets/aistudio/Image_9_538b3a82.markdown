## Brand & Style
This design system defines a high-end, immersive luxury footwear commerce experience engineered for iOS. It combines the rigorous precision of high-fashion catalog design with advanced real-time 3D spatial computing and Augmented Reality (AR) try-on capabilities.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Functional Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-clean, studio-lit environments where the physical geometry, textures, and silhouettes of luxury footwear take absolute priority.
- Restrained, razor-sharp typography with editorial letter-spacing that feels like an architectural monograph.
- Spatial tactile cues: frosted translucent sheets, micro-hairlines, and delicate floating reticles that ground the user during AR spatial alignment without obstructing the product.
- High-contrast, dynamic accents engineered to provide instant clarity in interactive 3D viewports.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a museum-grade neutral base with high-frequency spatial accents:
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F8F8`):** An optical off-white that prevents screen glare and renders shoe materials with natural photographic fidelity.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure light planes bounded by subtle structural hairlines (`#E5E5E5`).
- **Primary Ink (`#141414`):** Matte charcoal / deep ink ensuring maximum typographic contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Slate (`#737373`):** Subdued neutral for metadata, editorial specifications, and secondary actions.
- **Electric Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** High-energy primary accent reserved for focal checkout actions, active spatial anchors, and active selection state rings.
- **Volt & Emerald Accents (`#A3E635` / `#10B981`):** Micro-targeted neon highlights for real-time AR tracking locks, dimensional scale indicators, and limited-edition status chips.
- **Translucent Overlays:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` combined with backdrop blur (`blur(16px)`) and a delicate white edge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm follows architectural principles:
- **Systematic Precision:** Inter is utilized across all tiers, leaning heavily on tight negative tracking for bold display titles to evoke technical luxury.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** `label-caps-md` and `label-caps-sm` must always be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (0.12em - 0.14em). These anchor visual hierarchy in material callouts, technical specs, and AR UI tags.
- **Numerical Clarity:** All price points use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with medium weight to ensure clean vertical alignment across shopping bags and checkout summaries.

## Layout & Spacing
Built for iOS touch targets and edge-to-edge spatial viewports:
- **Canvas System:** A standard 4-column layout on iOS mobile viewports with `1.25rem` screen margins and `0.75rem` to `1rem` internal gutters. 
- **Safe Area Insets:** Floating HUD controls in the 3D Customizer and AR Viewport respect native iOS safe areas (top dynamic island/notch: `44px-54px`, home indicator: `34px`).
- **Aspect Proportions:** Product gallery cards conform strictly to 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratios, ensuring studio silhouette symmetry across varying device widths.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth hierarchy avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, leaning instead on tonal separation and optical refraction:
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F8F8F8`. Non-interactive flat surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Floating Sheets):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` structural hairline border of `#E5E5E5`. Ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Spatial HUD & Overlays):** Translucent glassmorphic pill panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), backdrop filter blur `16px`, and an internal rim light border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`. Shadow: `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets):** Elevates critical purchase flows. Pure white or semi-frosted sheet with a top handle pill and `0px -8px 32px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.08)`.

## Shapes
The shape strategy balances structured geometry with fluid human ergonomics:
- **Micro UI & Interactive Controls:** Buttons, chips, category selectors, and floating HUD toolbars utilize fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and modern mobile ergonomics.
- **Containers & Sheet Panels:** Product presentation cards and AR overlay drawers utilize `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem`), matching the native curvature of modern iOS displays.
- **AR Spatial Elements:** Target reticles, scale calipers, and 3D anchor points utilize pure circles and concentric arcs with 1.5px border weights.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Confirm Customization):** Full-bleed or pill-shaped, matte charcoal `#141414` fill with pure white typography, or Electric Cobalt `#2563EB` during key spatial conversion flows. Height: `52px` minimum for effortless thumb engagement.
- **Secondary Action (Enter AR Try-On / 3D Orbit):** Frosted glass background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), 1px `#E5E5E5` hairline border, deep ink `#141414` text, paired with vector spatial icon glyphs.
- **Ghost / Icon Floating Triggers:** Circular `44x44px` glass disks housing hairline vector symbols (gyroscope, reset angle, lighting toggles).

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Pill Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`. Inactive states feature `#FFFFFF` with `#E5E5E5` border and `#737373` text. Active states feature `#141414` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Limited / Edition Indicator:** Subtle `#10B981` or `#A3E635` dot indicator (`6x6px`) placed preceding micro-caps tracking text.

### 3D Customizer Carousel & Material Selectors
- **Material Swatches:** Spherical 3D material previews (`40x40px` circular badges) showing real-world texture rendering (e.g., tumbled leather, ripstop nylon, suede). Active selection has a concentric ring offset by `2px` colored in `#2563EB`.
- **Part Selection Pills:** Horizontally scrollable glassmorphic pill bar pinned just above the thumb zone allowing instant switching between "Vamp", "Swoop", "Outsole", "Laces", and "Lining".

### AR Reticle & Spatial Calipers
- **Foot Detection Visualizer:** Dynamic hairline reticle that pulses smoothly (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`) across the user's floor plane until anchor locking occurs, transitioning instantly into a static high-contrast `#A3E635` bounding contour with sub-millimeter tracking metrics displayed in `label-caps-sm`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Framed in pure white `#FFFFFF` with hairline `#E5E5E5` boundaries. Clean product isolation against pure studio void.
- **Metadata Layout:** Micro-caps category label at top left, heart/wishlist trigger top right. Bottom zone stacks the shoe silhouette title (`headline-sm`) over the monospace tabular pricing format.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Minimalist floating fields: height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, smooth transition to `1px solid #2563EB` upon focus. Inner placeholder rendered in `#737373`.
